Visas

Guests who aren't (overseas) citizens of India need a visa — a Tourist e-Visa is enough (30-day, 1-year or 5-year; apply up to 4 months ahead). Please ensure it's valid across Feb–Mar 2027. Apply at indianvisaonline.gov.in. You'll need a passport valid 6+ months, a digital photo, and proof of return/onward travel. For a visa reference, just ask Shreyam or family.

📝

Explore India

Many of you are travelling a long way — and February–March is a lovely time to see more of India. A few ideas to begin:

🔺

The Golden Triangle

India's classic circuit — Delhi (Mughal forts & bazaars), Agra (the Taj Mahal), and Jaipur (the Pink City; 2 days recommended): the best of the country's history and colour in a few days.

🏛️

Delhi

Old meets new — the Red Fort, Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb, India Gate, and the bustle of Chandni Chowk & Jama Masjid (best explored by rickshaw).

🌊

Mumbai

Seaside energy — the Gateway of India & Colaba, Marine Drive at sunset, the Elephanta Caves, CST station, and the cafés of Bandra & Juhu.
